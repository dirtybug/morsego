# Privacy Policy for MorseGO

**Effective Date:** September 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 20, 2026

MorseGO ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free and open-source application designed to teach and practice Morse code. We are fully committed to protecting your privacy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**MorseGO does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.**

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
- **No Analytics / Tracking:** We do not use any third-party analytics, diagnostic services, advertising frameworks, or tracking cookies.
- **No Internet Access:** The application does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and does not communicate with external servers or cloud services.

---

## 2. Device Permissions
MorseGO requests only the minimal permissions strictly necessary to operate offline:
- **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback (tactile vibration) when sending or receiving Morse code in silent mode.
- **`android.hardware.usb.host`**: Used to detect and communicate with physical USB Type-C dual-paddle Morse keyers connected via OTG. No data is transferred off the device.

---

## 3. Local Data Storage
Any progress data (such as unlocked training levels, preferred WPM speed, sidetone pitch, and keyer mode) is stored exclusively on your device using Android's native local preferences (`SharedPreferences`). This data never leaves your device and is completely removed if you uninstall the application.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy
MorseGO does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor does it collect any personal information from children or adults.
